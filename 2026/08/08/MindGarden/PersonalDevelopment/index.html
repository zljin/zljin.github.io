吉姆・罗恩《成功的挑战》的研讨会

Created2026-08-08|Updated2026-08-08|MindGarden
|Post Views:

refer：吉姆・罗恩《成功的挑战》的研讨会

Pay attention to personal development.
Because true happiness is not contained in what you get. Happiness is contained in what you became.

Find out how things work.

To change your life really, you need ideas. The major problem is lack an idea. Not a problem.

To get ideas, you need a constant study of finding out.

If you wish to be happy/success/wealth, Just study it.

Don’t leave it to chance.

If you find out sth that works, please note in your journal . Don’t use your head for a filling cabinet.

Put it in your journal so that you can do the next best thing. Repetition!!! Go over it.

How to get ideas?
1、Read, became self-educated.Thirty minutes per day.

Read something positive,challenging,inspirational,instructional

2、Listen

Go around successful people and listen.
You can also learn from unsuccessful people,and don’t do it. choose negative and positive.

3、Observer

Success leaves clues.
Sight and insight(see with your mind.) Best advice on this is pay attention.

Go to Work.

self motivation

Get excited over you ability to make yourself do the necessary things.
On any given day, you can massively change the direction of your life.

Don’t try to beat the system.

Find out how it works best and do it that way.
Please don’t look for cheap answers.
